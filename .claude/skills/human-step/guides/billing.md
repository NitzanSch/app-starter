# Add a billing account

Only use this after the user has agreed to it as a money question. Before they agree, tell them:

- which feature needs it, and that the alternative is leaving that feature out
- what Google's current pricing page says about the free allowance, checked today
- that budget alerts send a warning but don't stop charges

Steps:

1. Open https://console.firebase.google.com and choose your project.
2. Click **Upgrade**, or the plan name, near the bottom of the left menu.
3. Choose **Blaze (pay as you go)** and follow the steps to add a card. Enter your card only on Google's page, never in this chat.
4. When offered, set a budget alert. A low amount like $5 is a good start.

You'll know it worked when the plan name shows **Blaze**.
