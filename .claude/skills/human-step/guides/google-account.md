# Create a Google account

Why: Firebase, the service that puts the app on the internet, is part of Google and uses a Google account. It's free. A Gmail address already counts as a Google account.

1. Go to https://accounts.google.com/signup.
2. Enter your name and follow the steps to choose an address and password.
3. Enter the code Google sends to your phone, if asked.

You'll know it worked when you see your account page, logged in.
